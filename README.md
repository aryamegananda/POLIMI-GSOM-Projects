# POLIMI-GSOM-Projects
# Master’s Projects in Data Science & Business Analytics

This repository is a personal learning space developed alongside my Master’s program in **Business Analytics & Data Science** at Politecnico di Milano.

It contains projects and explorations created to better understand data, methods, and systems through hands-on work.  
The emphasis is on learning by doing: working with real datasets, experimenting with analytical approaches, and gradually building intuition across different areas of data science, business intelligence, and database management.

---

## Repository Structure

### 📁 Individual_Projects
Projects initiated and developed independently, outside of mandatory coursework.

These projects emphasize:
- problem formulation and decision-making,
- methodological depth,
- and end-to-end technical analysis.

---

### 📁 University_Projects
Projects completed as part of academic courses, including both individual and group work.

These projects focus on:
- applying course concepts correctly,
- working within given constraints,
- collaborating in a group setting.

---

### 📁 Practice_and_Explorations
Focused technical explorations to practice or deepen specific skills.

---
