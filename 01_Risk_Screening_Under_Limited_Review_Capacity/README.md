# Risk Screening Under Limited Review Capacity

🚧 **Status: Ongoing project**

This project explores decision-making under operational constraints in a fraud risk screening context.
The focus is on how limited review capacity affects fraud capture, cost trade-offs, and policy design.

This repository is a work in progress and will be iteratively refined.

---

## Data Source & Acknowledgement

This project uses the **Credit Card Fraud Detection dataset**, originally released through a research collaboration between **Worldline** and the **Machine Learning Group (MLG)** at **Université Libre de Bruxelles (ULB)**.

The dataset is widely used in academic research on fraud detection and imbalanced learning, and is used here **strictly for educational and research purposes**.

Relevant academic references are available in the original dataset documentation.
